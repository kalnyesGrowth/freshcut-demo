Built for Virginia's Long Leaf Season

In Fredericksburg's Zone 7a climate, leaf drop is not a single weekend. Oaks and maples let go in waves from early October through late November, and a thick layer of wet leaves left on the grass blocks sunlight, traps moisture, and invites lawn disease and mold over winter.

Fresh-Cut handles the whole cycle. We blow and rake the lawn, clear out beds and tight corners, pull leaf debris from your gutters during the season, and haul everything away. For heavy tree coverage, we set up repeat visits so your yard stays clear all the way to the first frost.

Leaf Removal FAQ

Common Questions About Leaf Removal in Virginia

When should leaf removal be done in Fredericksburg, VA?

Most leaf drop in this area runs from early October through late November. Because trees release leaves in waves, a single cleanup rarely covers the whole season. We recommend at least two visits, with a final cleanup in late November once the trees are bare, to get your yard clean before the first hard frost.

Why can't I just leave the leaves on my lawn over winter?

A heavy layer of wet leaves blocks sunlight and traps moisture against the grass. Over Virginia's winter this can smother the lawn, encourage mold and snow mold, and leave bare, dead patches you'll have to reseed in spring. Clearing the leaves protects the health of your turf.

Do you haul the leaves away or bag them at the curb?

We haul everything off your property. There are no bags stacked at the curb and no piles left behind for you to deal with. When we finish, the leaves are gone for good, not just moved to another corner of the yard.

Do you clear leaves out of gutters too?

Yes. During leaf season we clear leaf debris from your gutters as part of the cleanup, which matters in Virginia where clogged gutters lead to ice dams and water damage over winter. For full gutter cleaning outside of leaf season, we also offer a dedicated gutter service.

How many leaf removal visits will my yard need?

It depends on your tree coverage. A yard with a couple of trees may only need one or two cleanups. A property with mature oaks and maples usually needs several visits through the season to stay clear. Alex assesses your trees during the free walkthrough and recommends a schedule that fits your property.
