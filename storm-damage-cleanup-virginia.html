Storm Cleanup FAQ

Common Questions About Storm Damage Cleanup

How fast can you respond after a storm?

We prioritize storm damage calls and aim to reach your property as quickly as possible, often the same day for urgent hazards. After a major regional storm demand is high, so call as early as you can. We handle the most dangerous situations first, then work through the rest of the queue.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. We photograph all storm damage before and during cleanup so you have detailed records for your insurance company. While we don't file the claim for you, our documentation gives your adjuster exactly what they need, which usually means a faster and smoother approval.

A tree fell on my house. Can you remove it?

We can remove trees and limbs from your property, but when a tree is resting on your home or structure, safety and insurance steps come first. Call us and Alex will assess the situation, advise you on emergency tarping referrals to protect the interior, and safely handle the tree removal once it's cleared to proceed.

What about trees or limbs tangled in power lines?

Never approach a downed line, and don't let anyone else near it. Power line hazards must be handled by your utility company first. Report it to Dominion Energy or your provider, and once the line is cleared and confirmed safe, we can remove the tree and clean up the debris.

How much does storm damage cleanup cost?

It depends on the scope, from a single fallen limb to a mature tree across your driveway with widespread debris. Alex provides a clear estimate after assessing the damage, and we document everything for your insurance so you can recover eligible costs. Call for a free assessment and quote.
