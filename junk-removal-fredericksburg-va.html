Junk Removal FAQ

Common Questions About Junk Removal in Virginia

How much does junk removal cost in Fredericksburg, VA?

Junk removal is priced by the volume it takes up on our truck and the type of material being hauled. A single item like an old couch is inexpensive, while a full garage or estate cleanout is a larger job. Alex gives you an exact flat-rate quote before any work begins, so you always know the total upfront with no surprise dump fees.

Do you remove construction and remodeling debris?

Yes. We regularly haul away drywall, lumber, flooring, cabinets, tile, roofing scraps, and other construction debris from renovation and remodeling projects across Fredericksburg, Stafford, and Spotsylvania. Just have it piled up and ready, and we'll clear it out and clean the area.

Can you take a hot tub, shed, or old deck?

Absolutely. We handle bulky and demolition items including hot tubs, above-ground pools, sheds, and old decks. In many cases we can break the structure down and haul the debris away in the same visit, saving you the labor and the trip to the dump.

How should I prepare for the junk removal crew?

You don't have to do much. If it's easy for you, gathering items into one spot like a garage or driveway can speed things up, but it isn't required. Our crew does the heavy lifting and carries items out from wherever they are, including basements, attics, and backyards.

Do you recycle or donate any of the items?

Yes. We make a real effort to keep usable items out of the landfill. Furniture and household goods in good condition are donated to local charities when possible, and recyclable materials like metal, wood, cardboard, and appliances are sorted and diverted for recycling.
