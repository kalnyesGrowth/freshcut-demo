# Alex's 60-Day Google Review Sprint

**Goal:** 0 to 20+ Google reviews in 60 days.
Half of homeowners won't hire a landscaper with fewer than 20 reviews. This is worth more than any ad spend you could run right now.

---

## Step 1: Build Your List

Write down every customer from the last 2 years. Name + phone number.

Target: 30+ names minimum. Think back through:
- Regular lawn maintenance clients
- One-time mulch or cleanup jobs
- Tree trimming/removal jobs
- Gutter cleaning clients
- Storm damage cleanups
- Referrals from friends or family

Even if you only worked with someone once, if you did a good job, they'll leave a review.

---

## Step 2: Send the Text (5 per day)

Send these personally from your phone. Not a mass blast. 5 per day keeps it natural and avoids Google flagging a burst of reviews from one listing.

### English Template

> Hey [Name], it's Alex from Fresh-Cut Landscaping. I'm getting my Google page going and a quick review from you would mean a lot. Takes 30 seconds: https://g.page/r/CVVOks0d4bZgEBM/review . Thanks for trusting me with your yard!

### Spanish Template (Plantilla en Espanol)

> Hola [Name], soy Alex de Fresh-Cut Landscaping. Estoy activando mi pagina de Google y una resena tuya me ayudaria muchisimo. Toma 30 segundos: https://g.page/r/CVVOks0d4bZgEBM/review . Gracias por confiar en mi!

### Tips
- Send during business hours (people are more likely to act)
- Personalize when you can: "Hey Robert, it's Alex. Remember the tree job on Elm Street last fall?"
- If they don't respond in 3 days, one follow-up is fine. After that, move on
- Never offer payment, discounts, or incentives for reviews (Google policy violation)

---

## Step 3: The Forever Rule

Starting today, every completed job ends with this text the same day, while the yard still looks perfect:

> Hey [Name], thanks for choosing Fresh-Cut! If you're happy with how things turned out, a quick Google review would really help. Takes 30 seconds: https://g.page/r/CVVOks0d4bZgEBM/review

Send it within 2 hours of finishing the job. The yard is fresh, the customer is happy, and the review is almost guaranteed.

This becomes automatic when Fresh-Cut goes on the KG Reviews Engine. JJ will pitch this to Alex after approximately 5 completed jobs with review requests.

---

## Step 4: Respond to Every Review

Within 24 hours. By name. Mention the job.

**Good examples:**
- "Thanks Robert! Glad the maple trim opened up your backyard. Call anytime you need us again."
- "Appreciate you Sarah. That mulch refresh really cleaned up the front beds. See you in the fall!"
- "Gracias Tanya! Nos alegra que el jardin quedo perfecto."

**Why this matters:** Google treats review responses as a ranking signal. Personalized responses also build trust for anyone reading reviews before hiring you.

---

## Rules (Never Break These)

1. **Never buy reviews.** Google will suspend your profile. It's not worth it.
2. **Never review-gate.** Don't use a filter page that only sends happy customers to Google. This violates Google policy.
3. **Never write reviews yourself** or have friends/family write fake ones.
4. **Never offer discounts or free services** in exchange for reviews.
5. **Slow and real wins.** 5/day is the sweet spot. A sudden spike of 20 reviews in one day gets flagged.
6. **Never argue with a negative review.** Respond professionally, offer to fix the issue offline.
